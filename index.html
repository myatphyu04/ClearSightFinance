Welcome to Clear Sight Finance

Clear Sight Finance is a friendly guide to keeping your money safe. It is designed for people with sight loss and for anyone who prefers to learn by listening.

Scams are becoming more convincing, and anyone can be targeted. Knowing the warning signs, and what to do if something goes wrong, can help you protect your money and feel more confident.

The guide is split into three modules. Each one includes:

  • an audio lesson you can listen to
  • a written lesson content with sentence highlighting during audio playback
  • a few quick questions to check what you have learned

There is no rush. You can listen, read or do both, and come back to any module whenever you like.

Learning modules

You can work through the modules in order, or start with the topic that matters most to you.

  • Module 1 Spotting the Scam

    Learn the warning signs of a scam: suspicious messages, pressure to act quickly, people pretending to be someone you trust, and unexpected requests for your details or money.

    Start Learning

  • Module 2 Protecting Yourself Day to Day

    Simple everyday habits to keep your personal information, bank details and online payments safe.

    Start Learning

  • Module 3 What to Do If It Happens

    Clear, calm steps to take if you think you have been scammed, including how to contact your bank and where to report it.

    Start Learning

Clear Sight Finance is an educational website about financial safety. It is for learning only and does not give personal financial advice. If you are worried about your account, contact your bank using the phone number on the back of your card.

Audio narration uses your web browser's built-in speech feature, so the voice may sound different on different devices.
